\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Physics Benchmarks.} Most physics benchmarks grade final answers or
expressions~\citep{xu2025ugphysics, qiu2025phybench}.
\textsc{PhysReason}~\citep{zhang2025physreason} and
\textsc{PRISM-Physics}~\citep{zhao2026prismphysics} score steps of fixed problems against reference
solutions, with an LLM judge and with rule-based formula matching, respectively.
\ourdataset instead generates each engineering instance with its own reference, computing every
intermediate quantity exactly.

\paragraph{Symbolic Benchmarks.} Symbolic templates generate variants of existing problems to test
whether a model has memorized the original~\citep{mirzadeh2024gsmsymbolic, gulati2024putnamaxiom}.
\textsc{HARDMath} generates step-by-step solutions but grades responses with an LLM and
rubrics~\citep{fan2024hardmath}, and \textsc{FinChain} aligns a model's steps with an executable
gold chain~\citep{xie2025finchain}.
\ourdataset uses its templates for checking as well as generation: the code samples physically
grounded parameters and computes every intermediate quantity of the derivation, so the evaluator can
check each value a model states against an exact one rather than aligning or judging it.

\paragraph{Engineering Benchmarks.} General suites such as \textsc{SuperGPQA} test engineering
mostly through multiple choice~\citep{du2025supergpqa}, multi-branch benchmarks grade answers with
LLM judges~\citep{zhou2025engibench, naser2026eri}, and dedicated benchmarks typically cover one
branch, such as transportation~\citep{syed2024transportbench},
control~\citep{kevian2024controlbench}, electrical and electronics
engineering~\citep{li2025eeebench}, circuits~\citep{skelic2025circuit}, or thermal-protection
design~\citep{zheng2026tpscalcbench}.
Those that assess reasoning mostly use experts or LLM judges;
\textsc{ThermoQA}~\citep{duzkar2026thermoqa} checks steps against computed values, but on 293 fixed
problems.
To our knowledge, none combines physically grounded instances generated across engineering branches,
gold traces with exactly computed intermediate quantities, deterministic checks of those quantities,
and an evaluator validated against domain experts' step-level labels.

\paragraph{Step-Level Evaluators.} Correct reasoning steps require process supervision or reward
models that emulate it~\citep{uesato2022processoutcome}, yet process reward models are trained and
tested mainly on mathematics~\citep{lightman2023verify, zheng2024processbench} and perform poorly in
other domains~\citep{zeng2025versaprm}.
LLM judges make correlated errors~\citep{goel2025greatmodels} and agree with experts mainly on
questions they can answer themselves unless given a correct
reference~\citep{krumdick2026nofreelabels}.
\ourdataset therefore checks intermediate values deterministically wherever a check exists, gives
its judge each remaining milestone's expected value, and validates the full evaluator against domain
experts' labels.
