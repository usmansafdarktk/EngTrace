\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Engineering and Science Benchmarks.} Engineering and science benchmarks mostly score the
final answer or the whole
response~\citep{zhou2025engibench, naser2026eri, li2025eeebench, qiu2025phybench}.
Those that examine the reasoning have it rated by domain experts or LLM
judges~\citep{syed2024transportbench, zheng2026tpscalcbench, wasiq2026engvqa}, or check steps
against references on fixed problems~\citep{duzkar2026thermoqa, zhao2026prismphysics}.
\ourdataset evaluates the final answer as well as the derivation behind it against a gold reasoning
trace, rather than relying on a rater.
Because this reference is exact, most checks are deterministic and do not depend on whether a rater
could solve the problem.

\paragraph{Symbolic and Dynamic Benchmarks.} Fixed problems can also leak into training
data~\citep{zhang2024gsm1k}, so symbolic and dynamic benchmarks generate fresh instances from
templates, code, or perturbations~\citep{mirzadeh2024gsmsymbolic, zhu2024dyval, li2024gsmplus}.
Most of these generators, including those for
engineering~\citep{skelic2025circuit, chen2026optengine}, record only the final answer, and the few
that also write out step-by-step solutions do so outside
engineering~\citep{fan2024hardmath, imani2025sympybench, xie2025finchain}.
\ourdataset also uses templates, but each computes the full derivation alongside the problem, so
every intermediate quantity is known exactly.
Moreover, their parameters are physically grounded and realistic.

\paragraph{Step-Level Evaluators.} Step-level evaluation mostly relies on learned or judged scores.
Process reward models (PRMs) learn step correctness from human labels~\citep{lightman2023verify} or
rollout-estimated labels~\citep{wang2023mathshepherd}, which are noisy~\citep{zhang2025prmlessons}.
Trained and tested mostly on mathematics~\citep{zheng2024processbench, song2025prmbench}, they
generalize poorly to other domains~\citep{zeng2025versaprm}.
Reference-free evaluators rate each step's validity, informativeness, or
redundancy~\citep{golovneva2022roscoe, prasad2023receval, xia2024reasoneval}, and benchmarks with
generated solutions align steps or grade solutions with an
LLM~\citep{xie2025finchain, fan2024hardmath}.
LLM judges agree with experts mainly on questions they can answer themselves, unless they are given
a correct reference~\citep{krumdick2026nofreelabels}.
\ourdataset matches each response against the problem's known milestones and recomputes its
displayed arithmetic.
An LLM judge rules only on the unmatched milestones, given their correct values, so it never has to
solve the problem itself.
