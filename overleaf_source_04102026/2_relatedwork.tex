\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Physics Benchmarks.}
Most physics benchmarks grade final answers or expressions~\citep{xu2025ugphysics, qiu2025phybench}.
\textsc{PhysReason}~\citep{zhang2025physreason} and
\textsc{PRISM-Physics}~\citep{zhao2026prismphysics} also score steps against reference solutions,
with an LLM judge and with rule-based formula matching, respectively.
\ourdataset also scores steps against a reference, in engineering, and takes the reference from the
code that computes the answer, so every intermediate quantity is known exactly.

\paragraph{Symbolic Benchmarks.}
Static test sets leak into training data~\citep{zhang2024gsm1k}, and symbolic or functional variants
of existing problems test whether a model has memorized the original
instance~\citep{mirzadeh2024gsmsymbolic, gulati2024putnamaxiom}.
\textsc{HARDMath} produces applied-mathematics problems with numerically validated step-by-step
solutions~\citep{fan2024hardmath}, and \textsc{FinChain} pairs each templated financial problem with
an executable gold reasoning chain~\citep{xie2025finchain}.
\ourdataset carries this design to five engineering branches and draws its instances from a private
seed.

\paragraph{Engineering Benchmarks.}
General suites such as \textsc{SuperGPQA} test engineering mostly through multiple
choice~\citep{du2025supergpqa}, multi-branch benchmarks grade answers with LLM
judges~\citep{zhou2025engibench, naser2026eri}, and dedicated benchmarks typically cover one branch,
such as transportation~\citep{syed2024transportbench}, control~\citep{kevian2024controlbench},
electrical and electronics engineering~\citep{li2025eeebench}, circuits~\citep{skelic2025circuit},
thermodynamics~\citep{duzkar2026thermoqa}, or thermal-protection
design~\citep{zheng2026tpscalcbench}.
Of these, experts or LLM judges assess the reasoning in a few, and \textsc{CIRCUIT} instantiates
templates.
To our knowledge, none combines generated instances across engineering branches with gold traces,
deterministic checks of intermediate values, and an evaluator validated against domain experts'
step-level labels.

\paragraph{Step-Level Evaluators.}
Correct reasoning steps require process supervision or reward models that emulate
it~\citep{uesato2022processoutcome}, yet process reward models are trained and tested mainly on
mathematics~\citep{lightman2023verify, zheng2024processbench} and perform poorly in other
domains~\citep{zeng2025versaprm}.
LLM judges make correlated errors~\citep{goel2025greatmodels} and agree with experts mainly on
questions they can answer themselves unless given a correct
reference~\citep{krumdick2026nofreelabels}.
\ourdataset therefore checks intermediate values deterministically, gives its judge each milestone's
expected value, and validates the full evaluator against domain experts' labels.
\autoref{appendix:related} gives a detailed comparison and further related work.
