\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Engineering and Science Benchmarks.} Most engineering benchmarks grade final answers or
whole responses~\citep{zhou2025engibench, naser2026eri, li2025eeebench}; the few assessing
reasoning rely on domain experts~\citep{syed2024transportbench} or LLM
judges~\citep{zheng2026tpscalcbench, wasiq2026engvqa}.
In science, \textsc{OlympiadBench} and \textsc{PHYBench} score final answers or answer
expressions~\citep{he2024olympiadbench, qiu2025phybench}.
\textsc{ThermoQA}~\citep{duzkar2026thermoqa} and \textsc{PRISM-Physics}~\citep{zhao2026prismphysics}
check steps against computed values and reference formulas, respectively, but on fixed problems.

\paragraph{Symbolic and Dynamic Benchmarks.} Fixed problems can leak into training
data~\citep{zhang2024gsm1k}, so symbolic benchmarks generate instances from templates,
often from existing problems~\citep{mirzadeh2024gsmsymbolic, gulati2024putnamaxiom};
dynamic benchmarks generate them from code or
grammars~\citep{srivastava2024functionalbench, zhu2024dyval, mishra2024mathcamps} or perturb
existing problems~\citep{li2024gsmplus}.
\textsc{SymPyBench}~\citep{imani2025sympybench}, \textsc{HARDMath}~\citep{fan2024hardmath}, and
\textsc{FinChain}~\citep{xie2025finchain} also generate step-by-step solutions.
In engineering, \textsc{CIRCUIT} and \textsc{OPT-Engine} pose templated or generated problems with
their answers~\citep{skelic2025circuit, chen2026optengine}.
\ourdataset's templates encode the full derivation, computing every intermediate quantity exactly
from physically grounded parameters.

\paragraph{Step-Level Evaluators.} Process reward models (PRMs) learn from human step
labels~\citep{lightman2023verify} or from labels estimated by
rollouts~\citep{wang2023mathshepherd}, which are noisy~\citep{zhang2025prmlessons}.
Most use mathematical training data, where \textsc{ProcessBench} and \textsc{PRMBench} test them on
erroneous steps~\citep{zheng2024processbench, song2025prmbench}, and generalize poorly to
other domains~\citep{zeng2025versaprm}.
Reference-free evaluators score each step's validity, informativeness, or
redundancy~\citep{golovneva2022roscoe, prasad2023receval, xia2024reasoneval}.
LLM judges agree with experts mainly on questions they can answer themselves, unless given a
correct reference~\citep{krumdick2026nofreelabels}.
Unlike \textsc{FinChain}'s step alignment and \textsc{HARDMath}'s LLM grading, \ourdataset
deterministically matches stated values to its computed milestones and recomputes arithmetic,
leaving unmatched milestones to an LLM judge.
