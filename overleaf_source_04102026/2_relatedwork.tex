\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Engineering Benchmarks.} General suites such as
\textsc{SuperGPQA}~\citep{du2025supergpqa} and most engineering benchmarks, whether
multi-branch~\citep{zhou2025engibench, naser2026eri} or single-branch~\citep{li2025eeebench}, grade
final answers.
The few benchmarks that assess reasoning mostly rely on domain
experts~\citep{syed2024transportbench} or LLM judges~\citep{zheng2026tpscalcbench, wasiq2026engvqa}.
Closest to \ourdataset, \textsc{ThermoQA} in thermodynamics~\citep{duzkar2026thermoqa} and
\textsc{PRISM-Physics} in physics~\citep{zhao2026prismphysics} check steps against computed values
and reference formulas, respectively, but only on fixed problems.

\paragraph{Symbolic Benchmarks.} Fixed problems can leak into training data~\citep{zhang2024gsm1k},
whereas symbolic benchmarks generate fresh instances from templates.
Many vary existing problems to test whether a model has memorized the
original~\citep{mirzadeh2024gsmsymbolic, gulati2024putnamaxiom}.
Some also generate gold solutions, in physics~\citep{imani2025sympybench}, applied
mathematics~\citep{fan2024hardmath}, and finance~\citep{xie2025finchain}.
In engineering, \textsc{CIRCUIT} and \textsc{OPT-Engine} generate problems within one branch
each~\citep{skelic2025circuit, chen2026optengine}.
\ourdataset's templates instead span five engineering branches and compute complete derivations from
physically grounded parameters, so every intermediate quantity is known exactly.

\paragraph{Step-Level Evaluators.} These exact values still need an evaluator that decides which of
them a response reaches.
Work on process reward models centers on
mathematics~\citep{lightman2023verify, zheng2024processbench}, and such models transfer poorly to
other domains~\citep{zeng2025versaprm}.
LLM judges make correlated errors~\citep{goel2025greatmodels} and agree with experts mainly on
questions they can answer themselves, unless given a correct
reference~\citep{krumdick2026nofreelabels}.
The \ourdataset evaluator therefore matches milestones deterministically wherever a check exists and
gives its judge the expected value of each residual milestone.
To our knowledge, none of this work combines generated, physically grounded instances across
engineering branches, exact gold traces, and an expert-validated step-level evaluator.
