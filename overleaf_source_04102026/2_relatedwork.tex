\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Step-level evaluation in science.}
Most physics benchmarks grade final answers or expressions~\citep{xu2025ugphysics, qiu2025phybench},
while \textsc{PhysReason}~\citep{zhang2025physreason} and
\textsc{PRISM-Physics}~\citep{zhao2026prismphysics} score steps against reference solutions, with an
LLM judge and with rule-based formula matching, respectively.
\ourdataset also scores steps against a reference, in engineering, and takes the reference from the
code that computes the answer.

\paragraph{Generated instances with gold solutions.}
Static test sets leak into training data~\citep{deng2024contamination, zhang2024gsm1k}, and
functional and symbolic variants of existing problems test whether a model has memorized the
original instance~\citep{srivastava2024functionalbench, mirzadeh2024gsmsymbolic,
gulati2024putnamaxiom}.
Generation has also been paired with gold solutions: \textsc{HARDMath} produces applied-mathematics
problems with numerically validated step-by-step solutions~\citep{fan2024hardmath}, and
\textsc{FinChain} pairs each templated financial problem with an executable gold reasoning chain and
scores a model's steps against it~\citep{xie2025finchain}.
\ourdataset carries this design to five engineering branches and draws its instances from a private
seed.

\paragraph{Engineering benchmarks.}
General suites test engineering mostly through multiple choice; \textsc{SuperGPQA}'s 7,892
engineering questions often require calculation but are scored on the option
chosen~\citep{du2025supergpqa}.
Dedicated benchmarks typically cover one branch: transportation~\citep{syed2024transportbench},
control~\citep{kevian2024controlbench}, power dispatch~\citep{zhou2024elecbench}, electrical and
electronics engineering~\citep{li2025eeebench}, circuits~\citep{skelic2025circuit},
thermodynamics~\citep{duzkar2026thermoqa}, or thermal-protection
design~\citep{zheng2026tpscalcbench}.
Multi-branch benchmarks grade answers with LLM judges: \textsc{EngiBench}~\citep{zhou2025engibench}
with two LLM evaluators, and \textsc{ERI}~\citep{naser2026eri} against reference answers that an LLM
wrote along with the questions.
A few benchmarks look past the answer: experts examined the reasoning in \textsc{TransportBench} and
\textsc{ControlBench}, \textsc{ElecBench} scores its logicality, an LLM judge scores it in
\textsc{TPS-CalcBench}, and \textsc{ThermoQA} scores weighted solution steps against values computed
with a thermodynamic property library.
Fewer generate their instances: \textsc{CIRCUIT} instantiates circuit templates with several
numerical setups, and \textsc{OPT-Engine}~\citep{chen2026optengine} generates optimization problems
of controlled complexity.
To our knowledge, none combines generated instances across engineering branches with gold traces,
deterministic checks of intermediate values, and an evaluator validated against domain experts'
step-level labels.

\paragraph{Evaluators of reasoning steps.}
Outcome supervision can match process supervision on final-answer error, but correct reasoning steps
require process-based supervision or reward models that emulate
it~\citep{uesato2022processoutcome}.
Process reward models learn from human step labels~\citep{lightman2023verify} or from labels
estimated by rollouts~\citep{wang2023mathshepherd} and are evaluated on human-annotated first-error
benchmarks in mathematics~\citep{zheng2024processbench}, but those trained on mathematics perform
poorly in other domains~\citep{zeng2025versaprm}.
LLM judges are the common alternative, but their errors are correlated across
models~\citep{goel2025greatmodels, kim2025correlatederrors}, on difficult objective comparisons many
do little better than chance~\citep{tan2025judgebench}, and they agree with experts mainly on
questions they can answer themselves unless given a correct
reference~\citep{krumdick2026nofreelabels}.
\ourdataset therefore checks intermediate values deterministically, gives its judge each milestone's
expected value, and validates the full evaluator against domain experts' labels.

related work.
