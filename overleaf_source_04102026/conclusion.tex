\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced \ourdataset, a symbolic benchmark for verifiable process evaluation of engineering
reasoning, whose expert-certified templates generate each problem together with a gold derivation
in which every intermediate quantity, or milestone, is known exactly.
An evaluator that agrees closely with human judgment scores each response on its answer, the
milestones it reaches, and the arithmetic it shows, using deterministic checks wherever possible.
Final-answer accuracy barely separates the strongest models and conceals much of the underlying
reasoning: wrong answers often cover many of the milestones, while correct ones can contain
calculation slips.
Such slips, more than conceptual errors, account for most failures, and harder problems widen the
gap between stronger and weaker models.
Future work will confirm the principles and assumptions a solution applies, not only its numbers,
since a misstated rule behind a correct result escapes every numerical test, and will turn the gold
derivations into feedback for training and selecting LLMs.
Moreover, extending the approach beyond examination-style questions, to open-ended design, real
artifacts such as drawings and standards, and workflows that call solvers, simulators, and
handbooks, would bring evaluation closer to engineering practice.
