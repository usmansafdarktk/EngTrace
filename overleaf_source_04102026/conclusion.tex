\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced \ourdataset, 150 expert-certified symbolic templates in five engineering branches, and
an evaluator that checks final answers, intermediate quantities, and displayed arithmetic
deterministically wherever a check exists, with residual milestones sent to an LLM judge.
On the final answer the five strongest of eleven LLMs are near the ceiling of these problems, while
the process measures show how much of the gold derivation wrong answers keep and where correct
answers hide arithmetic slips; a rule misstated behind a correct answer escapes every deterministic
check.
Reasoning at medium effort lifts \texttt{GPT-5.4 mini}, \texttt{Gemma 4 26B}, and
\texttt{Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite}, which return no reasoning tokens by default, by 0.04 to 0.12.
The domain experts' reading of wrong answers finds mostly calculation slips, with conceptual errors
growing on harder problems.
Next steps are tests of memorized methods rather than wording, problems built on multi-modal
artifacts such as diagrams, and the open-weights models under tool use.
