\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The problems are examination-style by construction, as verifiable gold traces require, and cover
undergraduate curricula in five branches; ill-posed problems and real-world artifacts are out of
scope.
The main comparison is at matched settings, where three models reason at medium effort and the
endpoint of \texttt{Qwen3-235B-2507} offers no reasoning setting; the domain experts' readings of
the evaluated models and the further experiments are of responses at the providers' defaults.
The evaluator's agreement figures come from an expert study on five models that are, by design, not
among the evaluated eleven; on the evaluated models, experts read samples of the final-answer
verdicts, the judge's rulings, and the arithmetic flags (\autoref{appendix:readings}).
The final-answer check credits a correct value wherever it stands in the final answer and under any
of its unit factors; stricter scoring swaps only two models that the final answer does not separate
(\autoref{appendix:scoring}).
Milestone Coverage credits the intermediate values a response states and the judge rules reached,
so a valid route that bypasses a milestone is not credited, and it reads the visible response, not
reasoning text an endpoint returns separately; high coverage does not certify sound reasoning.
An LLM judge from outside the evaluated families decides 11\% to 24\% of milestones, depending on
the model; on sampled responses, a second judge from another family moves each model's coverage by
at most 0.019.
The arithmetic check and the judged step check flag slips with stated precision and do not catch
every flawed step, and a rule misstated behind a correct answer escapes every deterministic check.
Open-weights models are served by one or more providers at 8-bit precision or higher, and an
endpoint matched on at least 20 templates differs from its model's mean by at most 0.025
(\autoref{tab:providers}).
The further experiments ran on a 450-instance subset; the open book supplies the governing equations
without data tables, and the interpreter is offered to the two closed models, not required.
The paraphrase test covers the 114 templates that can be reworded without changing the problem, and
it tests memorized wording, not memorized methods.
Once public, the evaluation set can enter training data; the generator draws fresh instances from
new seeds, so a later evaluation need not reuse published ones (\autoref{appendix:statistics}).
