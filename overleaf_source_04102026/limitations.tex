\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

This work has several limitations, which future research can address.
First, \ourdataset is written in English.
Engineering is taught and practiced in many languages, and models may perform differently when the
same problems are posed in them.
Since the question text of every template is generated from code, the benchmark can be extended to
other languages by translating its templates and adapting units and notation to regional
conventions.

Second, the templates span several core engineering branches, and adding disciplines such as
aerospace, biomedical, or environmental engineering would broaden the benchmark further.
Because all templates share one format, new branches can be written and certified in the same way as
the existing ones.

Third, the problems are presented as text, while engineering work often involves diagrams,
schematics, and plots.
Extending the benchmark to problems that include such material would allow multimodal models to be
evaluated on it.

Finally, we evaluate a selection of LLMs available at the time of our experiments, and the results
reflect those model versions.
New models and versions are released frequently, and the benchmark and its evaluator can be applied
to them as they appear.
