\section*{Ethics Statement}
\label{sec:ethics}

This work uses synthetic data generated from symbolic templates and standard engineering principles;
no private, sensitive, or proprietary industrial data was used.
The domain experts who certified the templates and read the models' responses are not named, and
their individual labels are reported only as counts and agreement.
The results describe what LLMs compute on examination-style problems and do not support deploying
them in safety-critical engineering systems.
\ourdataset and its evaluator will be released under the MIT License.
