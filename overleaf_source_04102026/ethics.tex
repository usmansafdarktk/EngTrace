\section*{Ethics Statement}
\label{sec:ethics}

\ourdataset is synthetic: its templates generate problems from referenced physical constants and
engineering data, and no private, sensitive, or proprietary data was used.
Fifteen domain experts, three per branch, certified the templates and read the models' responses;
their names and individual labels are withheld, and the paper reports their judgments only as counts
and agreement.
The results describe what LLMs compute on examination-style problems and do not support deploying
them in safety-critical engineering systems.
\ourdataset and its evaluator are released under the MIT License.
