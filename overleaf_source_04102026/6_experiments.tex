\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Evaluated Model Suite}
\label{subsec:models}

We evaluate eleven LLMs: eight open-weights models,
\texttt{DeepSeek V4.1 Flash}~\citep{deepseekv41}, \texttt{Gemma 4 26B}~\citep{gemma4},
\texttt{GLM-5.3}~\citep{glm5}, \texttt{GLM-5.3-Flash}~\citep{glm5},
\texttt{gpt-oss-20b}~\citep{gptoss}, \texttt{Kimi K3}~\citep{kimik3},
\texttt{Muse Glimmer 30B}~\citep{museglimmer}, and \texttt{Qwen3-235B-2507}~\citep{qwen3}; and three
closed models, \texttt{Claude Sonnet 5}~\citep{claudesonnet5},
\texttt{Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite}~\citep{gemini31flashlite}, and \texttt{GPT-5.4 mini}~\citep{gpt54}.
None of them wrote responses that we used to develop or validate the evaluator
(\autoref{sec:evaluation}), and none serves as a judge.
Two flagships, \texttt{GPT-5.4}~\citep{gpt54} and \texttt{DeepSeek V4 Pro}~\citep{deepseekv4}, run
only as anchors on a fixed subset of 450 instances (three per template), which the further
experiments also use.
\autoref{appendix:models} gives the selection rule and each model's size and identifiers.

\subsection{Experimental Setup}
\label{subsec:setup}

Each model answers each instance once (24,750 responses) with the same zero-shot prompt and no
tools or retrieval, at its provider's default decoding settings and an output ceiling of 32,768
tokens (16,384 for \texttt{Muse Glimmer 30B}).
At these defaults, four models return no reasoning tokens (\texttt{Gemma 4 26B},
\texttt{Qwen3-235B-2507}, \texttt{Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite}, and \texttt{GPT-5.4 mini}, two of the
three closed models), so the defaults do not compare the models at equal reasoning effort.
We therefore also evaluate a matched configuration, in which \texttt{Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite},
\texttt{Gemma 4 26B}, and \texttt{GPT-5.4 mini} also answer every instance with reasoning at medium
effort (6,750 responses) and \texttt{Qwen3-235B-2507}, whose endpoint offers no reasoning setting,
keeps its default.
At the defaults, 260 responses (1.1\%) have no readable final answer and score 0, almost all of
them empty at the output ceiling.
The template is the unit of analysis, because a template's 15 instances and their gold traces come
from one procedure~\citep{miller2024errorbars}; every 95\% interval is a bootstrap over
templates~\citep{efron1993bootstrap}.
We compare two models with a sign-flip permutation test on their per-template
differences~\citep{dror2018hitchhiker} and correct each family of tests, such as the 55 pairs of
models in one configuration, with Holm's method~\citep{holm1979simple}.
\autoref{appendix:setup} gives the decoding settings, the prompt, and the statistical protocol.
