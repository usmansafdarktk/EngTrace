Engineering reasoning proceeds through derivations whose intermediate quantities can be checked, yet existing benchmarks such as \textsc{SuperGPQA} and \textsc{EEE-Bench} grade only final answers, while those that assess reasoning, such as \textsc{EngiBench} and \textsc{ThermoQA}, rely on large language model (LLM) judges or cover one domain.
To address this gap, we present \ourdataset, a symbolic benchmark for verifiable process supervision of engineering reasoning, with 150 templates spanning five engineering branches, 15 domains and 42 areas.
Each template is expert-certified executable code that samples physically grounded parameters, computes the answer and emits a gold reasoning trace from the same computation, so every intermediate quantity is known with its unit.
Model reasoning is scored deterministically wherever a check exists: final-answer correctness, reasoning coverage of the gold intermediate quantities, and the arithmetic of displayed calculations; an LLM judge from outside the evaluated model families adjudicates only the residue.
On 2,250 instances, eleven LLMs score 0.81 to 0.98 in final-answer accuracy, with the top five inseparable.
Reasoning coverage, however, ranks the models differently; models reach 46\% to 85\% of the intermediate quantities even with wrong answers, and every model fails most often on the longest derivations.
