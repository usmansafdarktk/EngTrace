Engineering derivations have checkable intermediate quantities, yet most engineering benchmarks
score only final answers, and step-level ones use large language model (LLM) judges or human
graders, or cover one domain.
We present \ourdataset: 150 expert-certified symbolic templates across five engineering branches,
15 domains, and 42 areas, each computing an instance's answer and gold trace.
Our evaluator deterministically checks the final values of six answer kinds (symbolic ones by
equivalence on five templates, by stated numbers on four), the gold trace's intermediate
quantities, and displayed arithmetic; a judge from outside the evaluated families decides what
matching misses and flags steps.
A misstated rule behind a correct answer escapes these checks.
We evaluate eleven LLMs on 2,250 privately seeded instances at the providers' defaults and three
of them with reasoning on.
On the final answer, the five strongest lie within two points, near the ceiling.
The process measures show how much of the gold route wrong answers keep, flag slips behind right
answers, and separate one tied pair.
Reasoning lifts those three models by 0.04 to 0.12.
Experts read 137 wrong answers and find mostly calculation errors; wrong formulas rise from 3\% of
Easy readings to 21\% of harder ones.
