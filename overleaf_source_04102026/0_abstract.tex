Engineering derivations have checkable intermediate quantities, yet most engineering benchmarks
score only final answers, and step-level ones rely on large language model (LLM) judges or cover
one domain.
We present \ourdataset: 150 expert-certified symbolic templates across five engineering branches,
15 domains, and 42 areas, each computing an instance's answer and gold reasoning trace together.
Our evaluator deterministically checks the final values of six answer kinds (symbolic ones by
equivalence on five templates, by stated numbers on four), the gold trace's intermediate
quantities, and displayed arithmetic; a judge from outside the evaluated families decides only the
quantities that matching misses.
A misstated rule behind a correct answer escapes these checks.
We evaluate eleven LLMs on 2,250 privately seeded instances, at the providers' defaults and, for
three models, with reasoning on.
On the final answer, the five strongest lie within two points, near the ceiling.
The process measures locate where wrong derivations break, find slips behind right answers, and
compare tied models.
Reasoning lifts those three models by 0.04 to 0.12, narrowing their gap to the strongest.
Expert readings of 137 wrong answers find mostly calculation errors, and wrong formulas rise from
3\% of Easy readings to 21\% of harder ones.
