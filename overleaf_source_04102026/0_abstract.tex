Engineering problems are solved by derivations whose intermediate quantities can be checked, yet benchmarks such as \textsc{SuperGPQA} and \textsc{EngiBench} grade final answers, and the few that assess steps mostly rely on large language model (LLM) judges or human graders.
To address this gap, we present \ourdataset, a benchmark of 150 symbolic templates spanning five engineering branches, 15 domains and 42 areas.
Each template is executable code that samples physically grounded parameters, computes the answer and emits a gold reasoning trace from the same computation, so every intermediate quantity is known with its unit.
Our evaluator is deterministic wherever a check exists: a final-answer check over six answer kinds, coverage of the gold intermediate quantities, and an arithmetic check of displayed calculations; an LLM judge from outside the evaluated model families adjudicates only the residue.
On 2,250 instances, eleven open and closed LLMs score 0.81 to 0.98 in final-answer accuracy, and the top five cannot be separated.
Coverage, however, ranks the models differently; models still reach 46\% to 85\% of the intermediate quantities when their answer is wrong, and every model fails most often on the longest derivations.
