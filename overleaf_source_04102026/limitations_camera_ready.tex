\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

This work has several limitations, which future research can address.
First, \ourdataset is entirely synthetic, and its problems are examination-style by construction.
While generating problems from symbolic templates gives each one an exact gold reasoning trace and
allows fresh instances to be drawn, the problems lack the open-endedness and artifacts of real
engineering work, such as ill-posed design problems, drawings, and standards.
Future work could seed templates with real engineering documents while keeping their traces
computable.

Second, \ourdataset focuses on quantitative derivations and does not capture qualitative aspects of
engineering reasoning, such as design trade-offs or judgments about safety.
Its deterministic checks also verify the values a derivation states rather than the principles
behind them, and checking those principles remains an open challenge.

Third, \ourdataset is limited to English and to curricula shaped by U.S. accreditation and licensing
standards, which limits its applicability to other languages and educational systems.
Extending it to other languages and regional conventions is an important direction for future work.

Finally, our evaluation reads the values and calculations that a response displays, so it depends on
how responses present their work, and it credits the gold route rather than every valid alternative.
Structured outputs or symbolic execution of responses could make these checks more robust.
