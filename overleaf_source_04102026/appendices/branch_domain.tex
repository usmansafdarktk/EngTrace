\section{Branch and Domain Performance}
\label{appendix:branch_domain}

This section gives, for~\autoref{subsec:branch_domain}, every model's branch and domain means
(\autoref{tab:branch_domain}) and draws those of four representative models:
\texttt{DeepSeek V4.1 Flash}, first on FAC and open-weights; \texttt{Claude Sonnet 5}, first on MC
and closed; \texttt{GPT-5.4 mini}, closed and fifth on FAC; and \texttt{gpt-oss-20b}, open-weights
and last on FAC.
The lowest branch differs by model, chemical for five models, industrial for five, and civil for
one, and a single branch pair differs within a model after correction, so the branch means carry no
order of difficulty.
In~\autoref{fig:branch_bars}, \texttt{DeepSeek V4.1 Flash}, \texttt{Claude Sonnet 5}, and
\texttt{GPT-5.4 mini} stay close to their FAC in every branch, while \texttt{gpt-oss-20b} is lowest
in civil engineering.
In~\autoref{fig:domain_radar}, \texttt{DeepSeek V4.1 Flash} is lowest on production and inventory
and \texttt{Claude Sonnet 5} on quality and reliability control, whereas the other two dip lower,
\texttt{GPT-5.4 mini} in production and inventory and \texttt{gpt-oss-20b} in geotechnical
engineering.
