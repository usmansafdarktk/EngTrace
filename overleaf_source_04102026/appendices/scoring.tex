\section{Scoring Details}
\label{appendix:scoring}

This section details the four checks of~\autoref{sec:evaluation};
\autoref{tab:scoring_settings} tests each setting on the expert study
of~\autoref{appendix:validation}.

\subsection{Final-Answer Check}

The check reads the final answer from the 700 characters that follow the response's last
final-answer heading or, failing that, its answer marker (the first when several lie within 700
characters of one another, otherwise the last) or its last mention of ``answer'', and otherwise
from its last 700 characters; a response with neither a heading or marker nor a number or verdict
word in its last 700 characters has no readable final answer.
It reads scientific notation, LaTeX, fractions, multiples of $\pi$, and thousands separators as
numbers, but not a digit subscripted with an underscore, as in \texttt{p\_1}.
The targets are the milestones on the gold trace's final-answer line, the last one for a scalar or
array answer and every one otherwise; where the line states no milestone, the target is its last
value that the question does not give, so a vector or multipart answer is then checked on that
value alone.
A classification answer also takes as targets any verdict word that its gold line states, which the
last non-negated word of the same family (e.g., laminar or turbulent) in the response's final answer
must match, and any yes-or-no property, matched label by label.

\autoref{eq:match} uses the unit factors $10^{\pm 3}$, $10^{\pm 6}$, $10^{\pm 9}$, $10^{\pm 12}$,
100, 60, 3,600, and their inverses, and $\epsilon$ is the check's one fitted constant.
A response's last digit counts only when one unit of it is smaller than the value, values also match
in absolute value, a question that prescribes its rounding requires the exact digits, and a scalar
gold answer stated in two units accepts either.
Every one of the 2,250 gold traces scores correct.

\subsection{Milestones and the Judge}

\paragraph{Milestones.} We rerun each template at the instance's seed and, if the regenerated
question and gold trace equal the instance byte for byte, take as milestones the computed values
that the gold trace states within 0.5\% and the question does not give under any unit factor.
We exclude values within $10^{-9}$ of 0, 1, or 2, bookkeeping variables, the intermediate states of
iterative schemes, and the elements of containers such as lookup tables, and count a quantity
stored twice once.
Matching uses the unit factors above except $10^{\pm 12}$, and MC leaves out the 70 instances
without milestones.

\paragraph{The Judge.} \texttt{MiMo-V2.5-Pro} (Xiaomi) receives one call per response in which
matching misses a milestone, with the prompt below, at temperature 0, with JSON output and up to
three attempts; it decides 294 of the 1,245 milestones of the expert study.
We credit only reached because the judge calls none of the 88 unstated values of
\autoref{tab:scoring_settings} reached but excuses a quarter of them as not needed.

\subsection{Arithmetic Check}

The check evaluates each chain of numeric segments in a step, such as $a = b = c$, with
SymPy~\citep{meurer2017sympy}, skips symbolic segments, and flags a result that is not a correct
rounding, at its displayed precision, of the value recomputed from the response's displayed
operands: $0.92979^{2/3} = 0.95300$ is flagged (0.95263 at five decimals), whereas
$0.92979^{2/3} = 0.9526$ passes.
Unit conversions are compared after their factor, and no result is held to a precision that its
operands cannot determine.
It flags none of the 6,875 calculations it reads in the 2,250 gold traces.

\subsection{Judged Step Check}

The check sends the judge one call per response with the prompt below, at the provider's default
settings, reviewing every non-empty step that the arithmetic check does not flag;
\autoref{appendix:validation} reports its validation.

CRITICAL RULES: \\
1. If the step is factually correct but takes a different path than the Ground Truth, you MUST use

2. Do not contradict the category definitions below. \\
3. The final output must be a raw JSON object only. \\

ERROR CATEGORIES: \\
- "Conceptual Error": The model applied the wrong scientific principle or formula. \\
- "Calculation Error": The model used the correct formula but made a mathematical mistake. \\
- "Alternative Correct": The model's step is valid and logically sound, but follows a different
method/phrasing. \\
- "Other": Nonsensical, irrelevant, hallucination, or formatting errors. \\

OUTPUT FORMAT: \\
Return a single JSON object containing a "results" list. Each entry must have exactly two keys:

\{"results": [\{"step\_index": \{first index\}, "category": "Alternative Correct"\}]\}
\end{tcolorbox}
