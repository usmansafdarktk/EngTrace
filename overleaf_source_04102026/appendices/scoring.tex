\section{Scoring Details}
\label{appendix:scoring}

This section details the four checks of~\autoref{sec:evaluation};
\autoref{tab:scoring_settings} compares each setting with its alternatives on the expert study
of~\autoref{appendix:validation}.

\subsection{Final-Answer Check}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Reading}: the final answer is read from a window of 700 characters that starts at
    the response's last final-answer heading or, failing that, at its answer marker, its last
    mention of ``answer'', or its end.
    A response with no heading or marker, and no number or verdict word in its last 700 characters,
    has no readable final answer.
    \item \textbf{Numbers}: scientific notation, LaTeX, fractions, multiples of $\pi$, and thousands
    separators are read as numbers; a digit subscripted with an underscore, as in \texttt{p\_1}, is
    not.
    \item \textbf{Targets}: the values on the gold trace's final-answer line that the template
    computed, only the last one for a scalar or array answer, and for an instance without
    milestones, the last value on that line that the question does not give.
    A classification answer adds its verdict word, which the last non-negated word of the same
    family (e.g., laminar or turbulent) in the response's final answer must match, and yes-or-no
    properties, matched label by label.
    \item \textbf{Matching}: \autoref{eq:match} with the unit factors $10^{\pm 3}$, $10^{\pm 6}$,
    $10^{\pm 9}$, $10^{\pm 12}$, 100, 60, 3,600, and their inverses, and $\epsilon$ as the check's
    one fitted constant.
    A response's last digit counts only when one unit of it is smaller than the value, values also
    match in absolute value, a question that prescribes its rounding requires the exact digits, and
    a scalar gold answer stated in two units accepts either.
    Every one of the 2,250 gold traces scores correct.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Milestones and the Judge}

\paragraph{Milestones.}
We rerun each template at the instance's seed and, if the regenerated question and gold trace equal
the instance byte for byte, take as milestones the computed values that the gold trace states within
0.5\% and the question does not give in any unit.
Values within $10^{-9}$ of 0, 1, or 2, bookkeeping variables, the intermediate states of iterative
schemes, and the elements of lookup tables are excluded, and a quantity stored twice counts once.
Matching uses the unit factors above except $10^{\pm 12}$, and the 70 instances without milestones
are left out of MC.

\paragraph{The Judge.}
\texttt{MiMo-V2.5-Pro} (Xiaomi) receives one call per response in which matching misses a milestone,
with the question, the response, and each missed milestone's name and value but never the gold
trace, at temperature 0, with JSON output and up to three attempts.
It decides 294 of the 1,245 milestones of the expert study and 11\% to 25\% of milestones on the
evaluated models.
We credit only reached because the judge never calls an unstated value reached but excuses a quarter
of such values as not needed (\autoref{tab:scoring_settings}).
The prompt is provided below.

\subsection{Arithmetic Check}

The check evaluates each chain of numeric segments in a step, such as $a = b = c$, with
SymPy~\citep{meurer2017sympy}, skips symbolic segments, and flags a result that is not a correct
rounding, at its displayed precision, of the value recomputed from the response's displayed
operands: $0.92979^{2/3} = 0.95300$ is flagged (0.95263 at five decimals), whereas
$0.92979^{2/3} = 0.9526$ passes.
Unit conversions are compared after their factor, and no result is held to a precision that its
operands cannot determine.
It flags none of the 6,875 calculations it reads in the 2,250 gold traces.

\subsection{Judged Step Check}

The check sends every non-empty step that the arithmetic check does not flag to the judge, in one
call per response with the question, the gold and response steps, and the indices under review, at
the provider's default settings; a step labeled a conceptual or calculation error is flagged.
The prompt is provided below, and~\autoref{appendix:validation} reports the check's validation.

\footnotesize
\ttfamily
You are an expert engineering professor acting as an automated evaluator.
Your task is to analyze the Student's reasoning steps against the Ground Truth steps. \\

QUESTION: \\
\{question\} \\

GROUND TRUTH CHAIN: \\
\{gold steps\} \\

STUDENT CHAIN: \\
\{response steps\} \\

TASK: \\
Analyze the Student Steps at these specific indices (0-based): \{indices\}. \\
Compare them logically to the Ground Truth. \\

CRITICAL RULES: \\
1. If the step is factually correct but takes a different path than the Ground Truth, you MUST use
"Alternative Correct". \\
2. Do not contradict the category definitions below. \\
3. The final output must be a raw JSON object only. \\

ERROR CATEGORIES: \\
- "Conceptual Error": The model applied the wrong scientific principle or formula. \\
- "Calculation Error": The model used the correct formula but made a mathematical mistake. \\
- "Alternative Correct": The model's step is valid and logically sound, but follows a different
method/phrasing. \\
- "Other": Nonsensical, irrelevant, hallucination, or formatting errors. \\

OUTPUT FORMAT: \\
Return a single JSON object containing a "results" list. Each entry must have exactly two keys:
"step\_index" and "category". \\
\{"results": [\{"step\_index": \{first index\}, "category": "Alternative Correct"\}]\}
\end{tcolorbox}
