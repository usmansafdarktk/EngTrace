\paragraph{Engineering.} Recent benchmarks extend coverage to agents working in professional
engineering software~\citep{gao2026engiworld}, strength of materials~\citep{wan2025som1k}, circuit
analysis from schematics with synthetically generated problems~\citep{akbari2025circuitsense},
telecommunications mathematics~\citep{colle2025telemath}, executable structural
analysis~\citep{qin2026structureclaw}, finite-element programming~\citep{mohammadzadeh2025fembench},
computational fluid dynamics~\citep{somasekharan2026cfdllmbench}, civil-engineering licensure
questions~\citep{silwal2026pecivilbench}, and process systems engineering judged by several
LLMs~\citep{sukpancharoen2026psebench}.

\paragraph{Science.} \textsc{OlympiadBench} pairs olympiad problems with expert step-by-step
solutions but scores final answers~\citep{he2024olympiadbench}; \textsc{SciDA} randomizes numerical
parameters at each evaluation~\citep{zhou2025scida}; and \textsc{NEWTON} generates
physical-commonsense questions from templates~\citep{wang2023newton}.

\paragraph{Contamination.} \textsc{VeRA} turns benchmark items into executable task
families~\citep{cheng2026vera}, \textsc{GSM-Plus} perturbs \textsc{GSM8K}
problems~\citep{li2024gsmplus}, \citet{chen2025staticdynamicsurvey} survey the move from static to
dynamic benchmarks, and \citet{xiao2026contaminationrankings} measure contamination as the gap
between original and paraphrased items.

\paragraph{Step Verification.} Error-detection benchmarks extend beyond
mathematics~\citep{tyen2023bigbenchmistake, zeng2024mrben, he2025deltabench, sun2026lsrben},
physics has a process reward model~\citep{dong2026physprm}, and GenPRM verifies each step with
code~\citep{zhao2025genprm}; reference-free step measures include ROSCOE~\citep{golovneva2022roscoe}
and ReasonEval~\citep{xia2024reasoneval}.
Closest to the milestones of \ourdataset, \citet{wang2026milestoneoracles} grade mathematical
reasoning against milestone roadmaps with a deterministic symbolic verifier, though their milestones
are written by a teacher model rather than computed by the problem's own code.

\paragraph{Judges.} \citet{thakur2025judgingjudges} and \citet{ye2025justiceprejudice} document the
unreliability and biases of LLM judges; judges accept valid outputs but reject few invalid
ones~\citep{jain2025agreeableness}, their agreement with human markers in physics depends on the
task~\citep{yeadon2026physicsjudge}, and they should be validated against human judgments before
use~\citep{bavaresco2025llmsinsteadofhumans}.
\citet{roytburg2026narcissists} re-examine self-preference findings.
\citet{jung2025trustescalate} escalate only uncertain items to stronger judges, and
\citet{huang2026verifierrobustness} ask a model only about answers a rule-based verifier rejects, as
the judge of \ourdataset decides only the milestones that matching misses.
