\section{Full Results}
\label{appendix:results}

This section supports the Results of~\autoref{subsec:results} with the measures in full and every
comparison behind them: the extended tables (\autoref{tab:results_answer}
and~\autoref{tab:results_process}), the pairwise comparisons (\autoref{tab:pairs_fac}
and~\autoref{tab:pairs_mc}), the branch, level, domain, and answer-kind means
(\autoref{tab:by_branch} to~\autoref{tab:by_domain_kind}), the level gap under its variations
(\autoref{tab:level_gap}), the sensitivity analyses (\autoref{tab:sensitivity}), consistency and
instance variance (\autoref{tab:consistency} and~\autoref{tab:instance_variance}), failure against
depth (\autoref{tab:depth}), the decoding repeats (\autoref{tab:repeats}), completion tokens
(\autoref{tab:tokens}), and the coverage and diagnostic details (\autoref{tab:coverage_wrong}
to~\autoref{tab:attribution}).
Every interval resamples the 150 templates, every family of tests is Holm-corrected, and every
non-significant difference carries the smallest difference the design detects at 80\% power
(\autoref{appendix:protocol}).

\subsection{Scores and Pairwise Comparisons}
\autoref{tab:results_answer} extends the main table on the answer side
and~\autoref{tab:results_process} on the process side.
Strict FAC, which scores a partial answer 0, changes no conclusion: the pairwise test on it agrees
with the test on FAC for 54 of the 55 pairs.
\autoref{tab:pairs_fac} and~\autoref{tab:pairs_mc} list the 55 pairs for each measure: 32 of the 55
pairs differ on FAC after correction, and 28 and 29 of the 55 pairs differ on MC under the sign-flip
and the Wilcoxon test; every non-significant FAC difference lies below what its pair detects.
