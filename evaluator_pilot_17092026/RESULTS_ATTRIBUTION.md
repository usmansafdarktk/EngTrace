## How to read the full run's attribution table through this

Q3's "what points at a wrong answer" gives, per model, the share of answered wrong answers with a digit-rule flag, an E5 MISSING milestone and a router-judge flag. The tables above say what such a flag is on labelled traces: the precision column is the share of flags that sit on a step the experts call incorrect, and the type columns split those by cause. A share of flagged traces multiplied by the component's precision is the share with a confirmed error of some type; it is never a count of calculation or conceptual errors, because recall differs by type and the conceptual steps are few (58 of 388 incorrect steps).
